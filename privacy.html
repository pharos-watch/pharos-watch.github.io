Pharos: privacy policy

For the Pharos watch face and the apps Pharos Hub, Pharos Weather, Pharos Time, Pharos Fit and Pharos Mind (Garmin Connect IQ). Last updated: 29 October 2026.

In short: no accounts, no tracking, no ads, no analytics. Nothing is sent to the developer. Everything the apps keep (your fitness log, saved items, settings) stays on your watch.

What leaves your watch, and where it goes

Only when a feature needs it, through your phone's Garmin Connect app:

FeatureSentTo
Weather (Open-Meteo, ČHMÚ or Yr source)your position, rounded to about 1 kmOpen-Meteo (open-meteo.com)
Weather (OpenWeatherMap source) and official warningsyour position, rounded to about 1 km, and your own API keyOpenWeather (openweathermap.org)
Tides (a tide field on the face, or Pharos Time's tides screen)your position, rounded to about 1 kmOpen-Meteo
Place name (the face's weather line, Pharos Weather)your position, rounded to about 200 m (or 1 km, setting)OpenStreetMap Nominatim
Near you and the country card (Pharos Mind)your position, rounded to about 100 mWikipedia and OpenStreetMap Nominatim

With the Garmin weather source the face sends nothing for weather; the watch's own Garmin weather is used. Pharos Fit and Pharos Time (except tides) work entirely offline.

These services receive the request like any web request (including your phone's internet address) and handle it under their own privacy policies. The apps send no name, account, device identifier or health data.

What stays on your watch

  • Pharos Fit's log (daily steps, heart rate, Body Battery and similar, from your watch) and its records, kept so streaks and trends can grow; removed with the app.
  • Saved quotes and items, reading positions, settings, the last weather and place name.
