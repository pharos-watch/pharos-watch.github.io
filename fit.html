Pharos Fit

Your watch measures a lot and shows little of it. Pharos Fit turns it into answers, on ten clear screens: should I push today, will my battery last until bedtime, is my heart rate telling me something?

Coming soon to the Connect IQ Store

Ten screens

  • Today: a 0-100 readiness score (our own estimate from Body Battery, resting heart rate, recovery and stress)
  • Body Battery: the last 18 hours and where it heads, “21 at bedtime” or “empty at 21:30”
  • Heart: resting heart rate against your own 30-day normal, with a plain warning when it runs high
  • Last 7 and 30 days, training by sport, VO2 max and fitness age
  • Totals: marathons walked and Everests climbed this year, best day, best week, longest streak
  • Battery, and your life in squares, one per year

Good to know

  • Keeps its own log on the watch, so records and streaks grow over time
  • Nothing leaves your watch
  • Not a medical device: the readiness score and warnings are guidance, not diagnosis

Elegant, rich in data and light on the battery. Designed for round screens, made for 70 Garmin watches. Free for now.
