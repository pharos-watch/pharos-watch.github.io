Home, three pages

  • Overview: rain coming or not, the next sunset and the time left, your readiness and where your Body Battery heads, today's question; steps and Body Battery rings at the edge
  • Day ahead: rain, sunset, golden hour, moonrise, tides, bedtime and when your Body Battery runs out, in one timeline
  • Brief: how you are, what the weather does, and the best time to train today

Inside

  • The complete Pharos Weather, Time, Fit and Mind, with all their screens
  • Touch and hold anywhere for a menu: jump to any screen, pin favourites to Home, change the weather source, pick your cities
  • Official weather warnings come first on Home and in the glance (with an OpenWeatherMap key)
  • With the Pharos face: hold the time to open Home; weather and health fields open their sections
