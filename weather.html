Pharos Weather

The question is usually “will it rain before I'm back?” Pharos Weather answers it on the first screen, then gives you the rest on six clean screens.

Coming soon to the Connect IQ Store

Six screens

  • Now: big colour picture, temperature, feels like, wind and gusts, and the line that matters: “Rain in 25 min” or “Dry next 2 h”
  • The hours ahead on one chart: temperature, rain chance and amount
  • Hours 1-6 and 7-12, then 5 days ahead
  • Details: humidity, pressure, dew point, UV, gusts, today's rain, sunrise and sunset

Good to know

  • Sources: Open-Meteo's best model for your area, ČHMÚ ALADIN, Yr / MET Norway, OpenWeatherMap (your key) or Garmin; switch on the watch
  • Official weather warnings at the top, with an OpenWeatherMap key
  • Fetches only while open: no battery use in the background

Elegant, rich in data and light on the battery. Designed for round screens, made for 70 Garmin watches. Free for now.
